# Business findings and recommended actions

*Basis: previously reviewed Power BI report values, not newly executed live Databricks queries.*

## Sales and profitability

Reported net sales are **$4.92M**, with **$2.02M estimated profit** and a **41.1% estimated profit margin**. Aligned year-to-date sales growth was **1.2%** at the report's latest sales date (August 2, 2026). Backpacking Packs led category revenue at approximately **$0.99M**, while Hiking Pants had the highest estimated margin among the five leading revenue categories at **45.1%**.

**Decision:** Evaluate revenue and profit rankings together. A high-revenue product is not necessarily a high-profit priority; Granite Pass Technical Storm Shell ranked fifth in revenue but second in estimated profit in the reviewed report.

## Inventory exposure

Reported total inventory was **$728,853.20**, including **$307,275.31 excess** and **$38,165.45 no-recent-demand**. Together these categories represented approximately **47.4%** of inventory value. Backpacking Packs accounted for approximately **$72.5K** of excess inventory. **Five** products met the reorder classification.

**Decision:** Review high-exposure categories for markdown, transfer, purchasing adjustments, or assortment changes while prioritizing the five below-threshold products for replenishment analysis. Product-level recommendations must be reviewed against warehouse-specific needs before operational action.

## Customer behavior

Of **1,000** customers, **840** were active, **111** at risk, and **49** inactive. **200** were high-frequency customers; loyalty membership was **33.5%**.

**Decision:** Explore targeted re-engagement of at-risk customers and assess whether loyalty and retention campaigns increase repeat purchasing. These are analytical opportunities, not experimentally validated causal effects.

## Caveats

Estimated profit excludes non-product expenses and is not audited financial profit. Inventory demand is relative to the last completed order date rather than today's date. Findings describe the supplied portfolio dataset and report; they are not claims about a real retailer's audited performance.
