# RidgeLine Outfitters — Portfolio Project 2 (planned)

**Working title:** Demand Forecasting & Operational Optimization

**Question:** How can RidgeLine turn the inventory and customer signals found in Project 1 into measurable operating decisions?

## Proposed scope

1. **Demand forecasting:** Build weekly SKU/category demand forecasts, with seasonality and a simple baseline comparison. Use time-based holdout testing and report WAPE/MAE plus bias.
2. **Replenishment scenarios:** Combine forecasts with supplier lead times, safety stock and target service levels to compare reorder points and order quantities. Include scenario constraints and stockout/overstock trade-offs.
3. **Warehouse analysis:** Extend the model to warehouse × SKU grain to evaluate stock imbalance, transfer options and capacity exposure; do not pretend the current product-level Gold table already provides this.
4. **Business impact:** Estimate potential working-capital release, stockout reduction and service-level effects under transparent assumptions. Show sensitivity ranges rather than guaranteed savings.
5. **Decision dashboard:** Create a Power BI planning experience with exception queues, scenario inputs and actual-versus-forecast tracking.

## Portfolio distinction

- **Project 1:** What happened, where are the risks, and what actions should be tested?
- **Project 2:** What is likely to happen, which intervention should be prioritized, and how could we measure its impact?

**Status:** Planned. No forecasts, experiments or optimization results are claimed as completed.
